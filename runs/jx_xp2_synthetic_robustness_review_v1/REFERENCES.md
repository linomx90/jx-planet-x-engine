# Literature positioning

Long-timescale Planet Nine dynamics, parameter grids, survey-bias-aware
inference, stability studies, and perihelion-crossing production are established
research areas. XP2 does not claim novelty for those scientific questions. Its
potential contribution is limited to a reproducibility-oriented combination of
an exactly matched deterministic factorial, a shared algebraic control, frozen
classification rules, replay checks, timestep sensitivity, and a limited
cross-solver sentinel.

The predecessor design cites Bansal et al. as a source for the broader
candidate grid. XP2's three-case diagonal subset, eight orientations, and equal
design weights are project-specific benchmark choices and are not attributed
to that paper as a probability distribution or inference.
