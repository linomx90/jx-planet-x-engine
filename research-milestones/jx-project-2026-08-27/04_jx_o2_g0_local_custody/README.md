# JX-O2 G0 local acquisition package

This package preserves already acquired public bytes and defines the exact
evidence needed to resolve the remaining JX-O2 G0 survey and matched-model
blockers. It is local custody and acquisition work only.

The preserved files remain quarantined from analysis. A matching SHA-256 proves
byte identity, not survey completeness, semantic adequacy, licensing,
independence, or eligibility for JX-O2. In particular:

- the public SurvSimB material is incomplete for the later OSSOS components;
- the retained A16 metadata ReadMe and HiLat source archive are explicitly
  unbound local copies that require authoritative reacquisition and license
  audit before any use;
- the explicitly withdrawn 2019 `Deep_Surveys` files are not preserved here and
  remain forbidden as activation inputs;
- the DES catalog and selection payloads still have unresolved release,
  exposure-count, band, efficiency, linkage, dependency, RNG, and license
  questions;
- the literature archives and Brown--Batygin orbit catalog do not contain the
  required physical source-population checkpoint or a paired M0/M1 model;
- the duration, numerical controls, survival rules, intrinsic population
  nuisance model, survey adapter, phase policy, and hardware determinism for a
  physical paired comparison are not locked;
- candidate 9118 remains retired for this experiment.

`local_custody_manifest_v1.json` records the exact local files.
`acquisition_checklist_v1.json` records every unresolved acceptance condition.
The registration binds the package only after the manifests and verifier are
complete.

JX-O2 remains `BLOCKED`. No GPU, dynamics, calibration, observed-data access,
model comparison, or Planet X claim is authorized by this package.
